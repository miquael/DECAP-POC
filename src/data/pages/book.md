When Leela inherits a box of temple photographs from her grandmother, she expects dates, labels, and a clean family record. Instead she finds gaps: a missing brother, a city no one will name, and a pencil sketch of Ganesha tucked inside a train timetable.

_The Threshold Atlas_ follows Leela from California to Chennai to a river town her family abandoned decades earlier. Along the way, the novel asks what it means to remove an obstacle when the obstacle is also a form of protection.
