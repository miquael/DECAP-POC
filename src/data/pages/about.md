Maya Raman is the author of essays, talks, and forthcoming fiction shaped by archival fragments, oral histories, temple art, and the everyday rituals that hold families together. Her work is interested in how people make meaning at moments of transition: departures, returns, births, deaths, vows, and unfinished conversations.

Before turning to full-time writing, Maya worked with community arts organizations and university reading programs. She now speaks with libraries, bookstores, and cultural groups about creative practice, sacred images, and the private notebooks that become public work.
