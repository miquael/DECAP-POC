There is a notebook by my front door because the most useful sentence of the day often arrives while I am looking for keys.

I used to think this was inconvenient. Now I treat it as a small ceremony. Before leaving, I write the sentence down, even if it is only a fragment. Especially if it is only a fragment.

Thresholds change the mind. The body has already begun the next task, so the imagination loosens its grip. Some of the best pages in the new book began as one line written badly, quickly, in that little notebook.
