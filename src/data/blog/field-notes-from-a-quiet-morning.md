This week I kept returning to the same image: a hand resting on a closed door.

At first it belonged to a character who was afraid to enter. Later it belonged to someone choosing not to leave. By Friday, the image had become neither fear nor refusal, but attention.

Revision often works this way for me. The page offers an image before it knows what the image means. My job is to stay with it long enough for the meaning to become more specific.
