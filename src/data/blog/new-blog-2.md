Last week's workshop took place after an early storm. By the time people arrived, the path had been cleared twice and the windows had gone silver at the edges.

We talked about the difference between a symbol and a detail. A symbol asks to be interpreted. A detail asks to be noticed first. The room understood this immediately. Someone described a brass lamp in her grandmother's kitchen. Someone else remembered the sound of a train before sunrise.

That is the pleasure of a good reading: one person's image becomes permission for another person's memory.
